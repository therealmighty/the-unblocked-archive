Save Data

Games keep your progress in your browser. If you clear your browser data, switch devices, or the site moves to a new web address, that progress can be lost. A backup file lets you bring it all back.

How to back up

  1. Press Export Saves.
  2. A file is downloaded to your device. Keep it somewhere safe, like a cloud drive or an email to yourself.

How to restore

  1. Open this site on the device or web address you want your progress on.
  2. Press Import Saves and choose your backup file.
  3. Confirm, and the page reloads with your progress back.

Good to know

  • It backs up game saves, favorites, recently played and your settings.
  • Importing replaces any saves with the same name on this device.
  • Some games save in other ways and may not be included.
  • Your backup file stays on your device. It is never sent to us.
